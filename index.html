📋 Rule sheet
The standing rules of the operation. Every rule carries the reason it exists, so it can be argued with instead of just obeyed.
—
—
Call days
1
June only calls on restock days. Calling on a non-restock day burns her time on numbers that haven't moved since the last round.
2
Restock is always Wednesday. Treat Wednesday as a guaranteed call day.
3
Restock is sometimes Thursday. Not guaranteed. Worth a round if Wednesday came up short.
4
Work the tiers in order — tier 1 first. The list is stored in tier order, so "next to call" already follows it. Tier 4 is hidden by default.
What counts as stock
5
A count older than 2 days does not count. Stale rows are shown, but excluded from the bar totals and from "most bars first".
6
"In stock" with no number is not stock. It is counted separately as "yes, no count" so it can never inflate the bar total.
Pricing
7
BUY is only ever June's price, or the online price minus $10 — whichever was updated more recently. Recency picks the basis; a day-old register quote must not outrank a Costco price refreshed this hour. Enforced in code by checkBuyPrice. Anything else is a bug and paints a red banner.
8
"Premium" means two different things — check the units. gold_prices.premium is a PERCENT over spot. The dashboard's onlinePrem is DOLLARS. Mixing them once invented a $7.10 gap with no error.
9
Always price off the most current gold spot. Spot is re-pulled on a timer and stamped with the time it was read. If the stamp is not recent, hit PULL before trusting a single figure on this page — every profit number is spot minus a discount.
10
Only flag a June-vs-Costco conflict if June posted a price TODAY. Her price is frozen at the moment she called; spot keeps moving. Comparing yesterday's shelf price against a live implied price mostly measures the overnight market, not a real disagreement — and sends her re-checking a store that was never wrong.
11
Elemetal pays spot − $20 for Pamp Fortuna, − $30 for any-brand bars (verified 2026-07-28 price sheet). The old − $10 ran unverified for weeks and overstated every profit figure by $10/bar. Condition: the Pamp rate needs Veriscan + green foot, or the bar counts as any-brand at − $30. Re-verify against each morning's emailed sheet — the rate is typed in, not fetched.
Cards
12
When Costco.com shows sold out, online-only cards leave the math. Mastercard (Citi Double Cash) works only on Costco.com, never at the register — with the listing sold out there is nothing it can buy. Its checkbox unticks itself and buying power drops it; the tick comes back on its own the moment stock returns. Your saved on/off choice is never overwritten.
