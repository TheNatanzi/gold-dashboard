The Trade

Buy PAMP gold bars at Costco on a rewards card, sell them same-day to Elemetal, keep the spread plus every reward.

LIVE
The whole trade in one line
Buy at Costco (bar price) → sell same day to Elemetal (spot − a small discount) → keep the spread + card reward + Costco 2%. The rewards are the real engine, not the metal spread.
What price to use (the BUY price)
✓Setting BUYR7
BUY = June's price OR (online price − $10) — whichever was updated more recently.
Recency wins: a day-old register quote must not outrank a Costco price refreshed this hour. Out-of-stock does NOT erase it — the last price stands until June or a newer lookup replaces it (or the 2-day expiry retires it).
Where the numbers come from: call days & June's quote → 📞 June call sheet · the Costco feed, staleness & the "premium" trap → 📊 Feed & data · what Elemetal pays → 💰 Buyers · which card to run → 💳 Cards & rewards.

?June vs Costco disagreeR10

The Lock

The "15-Minute Machine." Rides the price up, catches the best screenshot to forward to Elemetal.

LIVE
How it plays out
▶You tap START on your phone
It snaps the current price as the HIGH and starts a 15-minute clock.
Phase 1 · Grace (first 15 min)
✓A new higher price
Restarts the 15-min clock. Still grace.
✕Any dip — even big
Ignored. Nothing sends during grace.
?15 min pass with no new high
→ Enters Phase 2 · Sudden Death.
Phase 2 · Sudden Death (no timer)
✓Still within $10 of the high
Keep watching. Extends forever.
🔒Drops more than $10 below the high
LOCK & SEND — but only after 2 reads in a row confirm it.
↑A new high at any time
Throws it back to Phase 1 grace.
What the email does
✉When it locks
You get "🔒 Alchemy LOCK $X — forward before [time]" with 1–4 screenshots.
?Which screenshot to send
Forward the highest-priced one whose "forward before" time hasn't passed. You send it to Elemetal yourself.
Elemetal only accepts a shot under 15 min old; the machine only sends ones under 14 min (a 1-minute cushion). The machine never emails Elemetal.
Recipients: you only. Hamed is added only if you switch him on.
Safety rails
!The page shows a frozen / old timestamp
Throw the reading away. Never start the clock or lock on a stale feed.
Aug 18 bug: a 100-min-stale first read anchored the clock in the past → instant false lock + email to Hamed. This guard exists to stop exactly that.
Reads the Elemetal page every 15 seconds, only during market hours (Sun 5pm ET → Fri). Two watchers can never run at once.

Cards & rewards

Which card to run, how often, and the reward caps that decide it.

LIVE
Which card to run
✕Costco.com shows sold outR12
Online-only cards (Citi Double Cash / Mastercard) leave the math automatically — they can only buy on Costco.com.
The checkbox unticks itself and re-ticks when stock returns. Your saved on/off choice is never overwritten.
?Personal vs businessCC7
Personal card = one full run per statement period. Business card = multiple runs OK.
The run-frequency rule comes from the card TYPE, not a per-card list — any new card inherits it.
The gold card rotation
BUSINESSCiti Costco 6848 — Visa, in person or online. Multiple runs OK. Limit $15,000.
PERSONALU.S. Bank Smartly 4037 — one run per statement. Limit $10,000. 4% only on the first $10k of posted spend each cycle, then drops to 2% (CC5).
PERSONALVenmo 4334 — one run per statement. Limit $20,000.
PERSONALCiti Double Cash 2486 — Mastercard = ONLINE only. One run per statement. Limit $8,300.
NOT INChime 5316 — secured, $0 credit line. Not part of the gold rotation.
When a card is "ready" (CC1–CC3)
?A card has 3 states
In transit → posted → credit restored. Don't reuse until the payment is posted and credit is fully back.
A pending payment or a temporarily higher available-credit figure is not enough — the payment can still be returned or held.
✓Green "ready" needs 4 proofs
(1) statement period known · (2) no run already logged this period · (3) prior payment posted · (4) credit fully restored. Unknown on any = NOT ready.
Costco Executive reward math
Executive earns 2% back, capped at $1,250 / year per membership (≈ $62,500 of qualifying spend). The cap resets at the membership renewal date, not Jan 1, and counts purchases only up to 60 days before renewal. A capped membership is skipped until it resets — see 💳 Memberships.

Risk & taxes

The honest downside. Read this before scaling up.

INFO ONLY
Not tax or legal advice. General info compiled 2026 from public sources (IRS.gov, law firms, finance sites). Rules change and your situation is specific — confirm anything that matters with a CPA or attorney.
Chips: HIGH well-established · MED generally true, nuanced · LOW varies / uncertain · ASK A PRO get it confirmed for your case.
1 · Tax on selling the gold
?A fast flip is still a taxable gainHIGH
Held 1 year or less → taxed as ordinary income (10–37%). There is no "too short to count" exemption.
Gold bullion is a "collectible." Held over a year, long-term gains cap at 28% (a ceiling, not a flat rate). HIGH
!High-volume flipping may make you a "dealer"ASK A PRO
A profit-motive flipping operation can be treated as a trade/business (inventory + self-employment tax) — a materially different tax picture. High earners may also owe the 3.8% NIIT.
2 · Dealer reporting (1099-B)
✓A single 1-oz PAMP bar usually triggers NO 1099-BMED
Dealer buy-back reporting generally starts at 1 kilo (32.15 oz) or more of qualifying bars in one transaction.
!No 1099-B does NOT mean tax-freeHIGH
You owe tax on the gain whether or not a form is issued. This is where people get caught.
3 · $10,000 cash reporting (Form 8300)
✓Card & wire/ACH payments are NOT "cash"HIGH
The over-$10,000 rule covers physical currency + cashier's checks / money orders. Credit cards, wires and ACH are excluded (the bank already records them).
4 · Credit-card shutdown risk
!Buying to resell for rewards breaches most card termsMED
"Manufactured spend" generally violates the cardholder agreement — rewards are meant to come from ordinary consumer spending.
!The risk is the issuer's discretion, not the lawLOW
They can close every account, claw back points/bonuses (even after transfer), and blacklist future applications. Chase is described as zero-tolerance. Not a crime — a breach of contract with private consequences.
5 · Costco resale terms
?Buying for resale is conditionedMED
Members buying for resale are supposed to have a resale license on file and notify the cashier.
!Membership is "revocable without cause"HIGH
Costco can cancel any membership at any time. Practical risk: losing the buying channel. Keep purchase records.
